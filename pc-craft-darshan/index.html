How it works

Four steps from intention to invoice-ready build list.

  1. 01

    Choose your use case

    Gaming, video editing, office work, or general use — your workflow determines the optimal component balance.

  2. 02

    Set your budget

    Drag the slider to your total budget in INR. We'll work within it, allocating spend intelligently across components.

  3. 03

    Configure preferences

    Brand preferences, form factor, upgrade path priority — fine-tune the recommendation engine to your needs.

  4. 04

    Get your build

    Receive a full component list with individual and total pricing, GST included, ready to share or save.
