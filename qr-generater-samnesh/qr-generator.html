▸ How It Works

This tool generates QR codes entirely in your browser using a pure JavaScript implementation of the QR Code specification (ISO/IEC 18004). No data ever leaves your device.

The algorithm encodes your input into a binary matrix, applies Reed-Solomon error correction (at the level you choose), then masks the data for optimal scanner readability.
